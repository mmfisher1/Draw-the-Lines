Field Guide

Vocabulary in action

Census counts people. Reapportionment redistributes House seats among states. Redistricting draws boundaries within states.

Contiguity means all parts of a district connect. Cracking disperses an opposing group; packing concentrates one. A map’s appearance alone does not establish intent.

Simulation assumptions

All data, parties, articles, testimony and people are fictional. Each block stands for 100,000 residents. The nationally determined seat gain is assumed, not calculated from Merritt’s growth alone. The classroom model simplifies real districting legal requirements.

Fictional civic learning simulation. Maps and modeled election outcomes are illustrative and not legal determinations. No student information is sent to a server; progress is saved in this browser.
